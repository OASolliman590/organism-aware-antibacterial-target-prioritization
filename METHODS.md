## Interpretation

This framework generates ranked, organism-aware antibacterial target hypotheses from public chemical and biological evidence. Its similarity, heuristic prioritization, confidence, and applicability-domain outputs must not be represented as experimental proof of binding, mechanism of action, antibacterial activity, MIC, toxicity, or clinical efficacy. Only fields explicitly generated by an appropriately held-out calibration procedure may be interpreted probabilistically.
